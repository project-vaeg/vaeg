# M65j maintainer amendment

The exact 19 M65j selector groups and 5,908 hashes remain owned by M65j, but
their gap kind is corrected from `implementation_missing` to
`target_support_unverified`. Each group is `approved_nonblocking_defer` because
positive uPD9002 target authority and an executable target contract are
unavailable. Selected/applicable sets and all SST results remain unchanged.
This amendment applies only to the committed 19 groups and does not authorize
any other selector or zero-coverage family. No semantic implementation is
permitted. Formal approval remains deferred to G65m.
